# LTResearch

**A public research hub that supports LTRCSMU ("LTR" for short), under Love Transcends.**

LTResearch collects safe, public, well-cited material that helps the IAGuardian research agents and the people who work with them. It is intentionally basic for now. It will grow one small, sourced piece at a time.

> ## ⚠️ Read this first
> - **Educational only. Not financial, legal, or tax advice.**
> - **Testnet only.** Anything in this repo about tokens or chains is for public test networks. **Testnet tokens have no value.**
> - **Use at your own risk.** Nothing here promises profit, income, or returns of any kind.
> - **Paper mode by default.** Trading and signal ideas here are simulation and notification only. A human decides and signs every real action.
> - **Never share or commit** private keys, seed phrases, API keys, or bot tokens.

## Status labels used in this repo

- **Verified**: the link was opened and the license was checked on the date shown.
- **Unverified**: not yet checked. Treat with care.
- **DRAFT / roadmap**: an idea or plan. It is not a finished, working product.

## How to contribute (short version)

1. Open an issue using the **Research contribution** template.
2. Cite every source. Mark AI assistance (see [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](ATTRIBUTION.md)).
3. Keep it publicly safe: no secrets, no private files, no personal data, no third-party code without a license and credit.

Full steps are in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Attribution policy (short version)

Cite everything. If AI produced **51% or more** of a finding, mark it `ai-assisted: yes`. Below 51%, marking is optional but welcome. Details and a template are in [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](ATTRIBUTION.md).
